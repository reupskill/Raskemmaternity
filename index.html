FREQUENTLY ASKED

Questions, answered

Are you open 24 hours?

+
Yes. Our maternity home is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Emergencies and deliveries are welcome at any time. Just call ahead if you can.

Where are you located?

+
We are at Tudunwada, White House Line, Via FHA, Lugbe, Abuja, FCT. It's easy to reach from around the Lugbe area.

Do I need an appointment for ante-natal care?

+
Walk-ins are welcome, but calling ahead on 0811 103 6705 helps us prepare for your visit and reduces your waiting time.

Is HIV counseling and testing confidential?

+
Absolutely. All counseling and testing is completely confidential and handled with care and respect.

What should I bring for delivery?

+
Bring your ante-natal records, personal items for you and the baby, and any prescribed medication. Our team will guide you through the rest.

Can my partner or a relative stay with me?

+
Yes. We understand how important support is during pregnancy and delivery, so a partner or close relative is welcome to be with you.

How much do your services cost?

+
Our fees are kept fair and affordable. Costs depend on the service you need, so please call us on 0811 103 6705 and we will gladly explain the options.

What care do you offer after my baby is born?

+
We provide post-natal check-ups for mother and baby, feeding and newborn-care guidance, and family planning advice whenever you are ready.

What happens if I need specialist care?

+
If a case needs specialist attention beyond our home, our team will stabilise you and arrange a prompt referral to an appropriate hospital.
ABOUT US

Caring for mothers & families in Abuja

Since 2017, Raskem Maternity Home has been providing trusted maternal and healthcare services to the rural community of Tudun Wada, Lugbe, Abuja. We offer antenatal care, delivery, family planning, ultrasound scanning, HIV counseling and testing, and general medical consultations—combining professional clinical care with the warmth and compassion every family deserves.
