ABOUT US

Caring for mothers & families in Abuja

Raskem Maternity Homes is a maternity home & health care service based in Tudunwada, Lugbe, Abuja. We provide ante-natal, delivery, family planning, scanning, HIV counseling & testing, and general consultancy. We combine professional, clinical standards with the warmth every family deserves.
